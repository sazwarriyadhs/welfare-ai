# Drone Intelligence - Industry Use Cases

## 1. Dairy

- Aerial cow detection and counting.
- Herd distribution monitoring.
- Thermal image review where suitable.
- Pasture and feed-area mapping.
- Farm facility inspection.
- Historical aerial welfare evidence.

Recommended categories:
- Enterprise Quadcopter.
- Multispectral Drone.
- VTOL Fixed-Wing for extensive properties.

## 2. Sheep

- Flock detection and counting.
- Flock distribution observation.
- Remote pasture monitoring.
- Terrain and paddock mapping.
- Identification of unusual group distribution for human review.

Recommended categories:
- Enterprise Quadcopter.
- VTOL Fixed-Wing for large properties.
- Multispectral Drone for pasture intelligence.

## 3. Beef

- Cattle detection and counting.
- Herd distribution observation.
- Paddock and water-point monitoring.
- Pasture mapping.
- Remote facility inspection.

Recommended categories:
- Enterprise Quadcopter.
- VTOL Fixed-Wing.
- Multispectral Drone.

## 4. Livestock Transport

Potential drone-assisted use cases:
- External facility and yard observation.
- Loading-area visual inspection.
- Arrival-area environmental overview.
- Geospatial documentation of approved operational areas.

Drone use must not interfere with vehicle operations,
animal handling or transport safety.

Drone-based observation is not a substitute for direct
fitness-for-transport inspection.

## 5. Slaughterhouse

Potential drone-assisted use cases:
- External facility perimeter observation.
- Roof and infrastructure inspection.
- Outdoor holding-area overview.
- Environmental and site documentation.
- Approved aerial evidence collection.

Drone imagery is not a substitute for ante-mortem inspection,
direct animal handling assessment, stunning verification
or required slaughterhouse welfare procedures.

## Industry Integration Principle

Drone intelligence complements the existing welfare workflow.
It does not replace physical inspections or authorised personnel.

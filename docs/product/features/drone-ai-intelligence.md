# Drone AI Intelligence

## Computer Vision

Potential capabilities:
- Animal detection.
- Animal counting.
- Species classification where validated.
- Herd distribution analysis.
- Animal movement indicators.
- Image quality assessment.

## Thermal Intelligence

Potential capabilities:
- Thermal image ingestion.
- Thermal pattern visualisation.
- Potential anomaly flagging.
- Historical comparison.

No veterinary diagnosis is produced solely from drone thermal data.

## Pasture Intelligence

Potential capabilities:
- Vegetation segmentation.
- Multispectral image processing.
- Vegetation index calculation where calibration permits.
- Pasture change detection.
- Spatial vegetation summaries.

## Geospatial Intelligence

- Orthomosaic and map layer integration.
- Farm and paddock overlays.
- Georeferenced image management.
- Coverage visualisation.
- Spatial evidence references.

## AI Governance

Every AI result should record:
- Model name and version.
- Processing timestamp.
- Input evidence reference.
- Confidence where applicable.
- Processing configuration.
- Human review state.
- Reviewer decision and timestamp.

## Validation

Models must be evaluated against relevant species,
breed, environment, flight altitude, image resolution,
lighting, camera and operating conditions.

AI indicators must not be represented as confirmed
injury, disease or legal non-compliance without
appropriate assessment.
